About Engineer's Tools

Engineer's Tools is the English edition of Ferramentas do Engenheiro, a Brazilian site of free engineering calculators and exam practice for engineers, technicians and students.

Every calculator is written to be checked: it shows the formula, the units, the reference standard and the intermediate steps. Results are tested against hand calculations and published tables before going live.

Corrections and suggestions

Found an error or want a new calculator? Send a direct message to @ferramentasdoengenheiro on Instagram with the page link, the values you entered and the result you expected.

The tools support study, estimating and preliminary design. They do not replace the work and responsibility of a licensed professional engineer.
